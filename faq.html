Is registration still open?

The registration window is now closed. If you are still interested in joining, email csqa.gujarat@gmail.com. The CSQA Team will contact you if a seat becomes available.

When do I pay the fee?

Only after the CSQA Team selects your profile and invites you to register. Nothing is charged when you submit your interest.

Why is there a fee?

The nominal fee helps confirm committed attendance, plan the event logistics responsibly, and deliver the best possible workshop experience for every attendee.

How many seats are there?

We are planning for 50–75 participants. Capacity may increase only if the arrangements allow us to maintain a high-quality experience. Registration is currently closed.

02
Preparation

Before you come

Do I need coding experience?

No. Teams bring together different roles and experience levels, and no prior AI Agent-building experience is required.

What tools do I need installed?

Bring a laptop. We will share the required AI access, software, and setup steps before the event. Your designated CSQA volunteer will help resolve any readiness blockers.

Can I come with my own team?

Coming with colleagues does not affect team formation. The CSQA Group will assign teams based on participants' experience and expertise to create balanced, effective groups.

03
Workshop day

Building together

What will we build?

Each team will receive an assigned practical problem and build an AI Agent during the three-hour build session. The What You'll Build page shows illustrative examples.

Will sessions be recorded?

The opening session may be recorded. Build time will not be recorded.

How are demo teams selected?

Facilitators will select two teams based on how effectively their Agent addresses the assigned problem and how clearly the team presents its work.
