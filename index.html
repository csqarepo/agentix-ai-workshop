Example problem statements

Refinement Agent

Turns rough tickets into well-formed stories with acceptance criteria.

in: "login is slow sometimes"
out: story + AC + open questions
✅

Test Case Creation Agent

Writes structured test cases from a user story or spec.

in: user story
out: positive, negative & edge cases
🐞

Bug Analysis Agent

Triages bug reports and suggests likely root causes and next steps.

in: bug report + logs
out: severity, likely cause, next step

These are examples of common agent use cases. The session focuses on the approach, not a deep dive into each.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Do I need coding experience?

No. Teams mix roles, and the approach works for developers, QA and product folks alike.

Can I come with my own team?

Mention your teammates in the registration and we'll try to seat you together. Teams are finalized in advance.

What tools do I need installed?

A laptop with access to Codex or Claude. We'll send setup steps before the event.

Will sessions be recorded?

The opening session may be recorded. Build time won't be.

How are demo teams selected?

Facilitators pick two teams based on how well their agent solves the problem and how clearly they can show it.
